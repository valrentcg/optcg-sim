# OPTCG Sim v1.0.27

## Playing an Event or Counter
- **Events and Counters now burn away centre-screen.** The card travels out of your hand to the middle of the screen, holds long enough to read, and incinerates — so both players can see what was used instead of a card quietly vanishing from hand.
- **It reforms into your trash pile** as the burn finishes, rather than sliding there as a separate animation competing with it.
- **The burn takes its colour from the card.** Red burns like fire, blue like frost, purple like void, green jade, yellow gold, black ash — driven by the card's colour, not a per-card setting.
- **Counters played from the counter panel show it too.** Anything with an effect (Love-Love Beam and the like) is played from that panel rather than by clicking the card in hand, and was previously spent with no feedback at all.
- **Events no longer draw a targeting arrow while you drag them.** There is nothing to aim at — drag up and release to play, or drag back into your hand to cancel. Characters and Stages keep their arrow, because those do pick a slot.

## Your own DON!!
- **A DON!! DECK button on the deck-select screen** lets you choose the art your DON!! wear.
- **Thirty alternate DON!! from PRB01 are included.** Use the stock art, put a single art on all ten, or set each of the ten individually.
- **Your choice travels with you** — your opponent sees your DON!! the way you built them. Anyone missing an art file simply sees the standard DON!!.

## Sealed / Pre-Release
- **SKIP PACK as well as SKIP ALL.** Rush a single pack without giving up the rest of the ceremony. A skipped pack still lays out everything that was in it — it skips the ceremony, not the information.
- **Every Sealed screen can reach the menu.** The loading screen, the pack opening and the deck builder had no exit; an illegal pool could leave you stuck in the builder with no way back. Leaving is safe — the run is saved and resumes where you left it.

## Fixes
- **Gum-Gum Champion Rifle no longer freezes the game.** Played as a Counter with no opposing Characters to return, its mandatory "return 1 of their active Characters" waited on a choice that could never be made. The power boost still applies and the impossible clause is skipped, as the comprehensive rules require. The same guard covers Chambres.
- **Cards always draw in front of the field zones.** DON!! attached to a Character hang below and beside it, and were being clipped by the stage and leader panels they overlapped.
- **The DON!! card front no longer shows the card back.** An art update had replaced the front image.
- **Sandbox is labelled as in development**, alongside Puzzles and Sealed.

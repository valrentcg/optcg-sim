# One Piece TCG Simulator 1.0.37

## Play menu

- Reworked the Play screen around your selected Duel deck and the Solo Play choices that are available in the game. Casual, Ranked, Custom, Versus Self, Versus A.I., and Sealed have clearer entry points.
- Selected Leader cards use the game's rounded corners, angled card art, and a softer illustration backdrop. Solo seats are labeled Your Deck and Bot's Deck.
- Casual and Ranked show live players in queue and players in matches when the population service is available.

## Friends and chat

- Search and scroll your Friends list. Friend requests and invitations are easier to find, and removing or blocking a friend asks for confirmation.
- Chat loads recent messages first and can page back through older messages. Drafts and input focus survive menu refreshes; a failed request no longer clears unread badges.
- Create a customizable private room for a friend directly from Friends and send the invite with room creation. Hosts can also invite a friend or retry a failed invite from the waiting room.

## Custom rooms

- Create Room, Join by Code, and Find a Table have their own sections. Drag the Find a Table divider to resize the browser.
- Public rooms show the host, occupancy, selected Leader, and rules. Search by room name, host, Leader name, or Leader card code.
- The waiting room shows both players side by side with names, profile pictures, selected decks or Leaders, and ready status.
- Sealed setup shows its set, timing, and forgiveness controls without Constructed-only rules.

## Live status

- The header shows network availability, online players, and players registered since live tracking began. Unavailable service data is shown as unavailable rather than a fabricated zero.
- Queue and match counts use recent authenticated activity. Players on older builds will not appear in the new live online count until they update.

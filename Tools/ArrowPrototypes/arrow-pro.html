Arrow · to the style guides

Every control here maps to a published principle, cited below it.

Edge quality

A crisp core edge against a soft halo. All-soft is the main reason an effect reads as unfinished.

"Always hand-paint shapes using a mixture of soft and hard edges." — League of Legends VFX Style Guide

Value & composition

Brightness rises toward the target so the eye lands on the decision, and the tail fades out rather than ending on a cut.

Composition guides "the viewer's eye to the most important part." — VFX Apprentice

Silhouette

"Shapes are the primary way to communicate to the gamer." — VFX Apprentice

Colour — gradient map

A greyscale cross-section drives a gradient lookup, so colour is authored as a ramp instead of hardcoded. Saturation is kept off both extremes.

"Avoid 100% or 0% saturation." — LoL VFX Style Guide · gradient mapping per Alisavakis

Surface

Slow variation along the length, standing in for a hand-painted texture. Without it the ribbon is machine-uniform, which is a large part of the sterile look.
